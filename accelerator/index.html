The problem

Small venues face a fragmented digital stack.

Setup friction

Menus and service catalogues live in PDFs, photos and disconnected tools.

Operational burden

Menu edits, languages and staff workflows require repeated manual work.

Customer friction

Guests navigate different interfaces for menu discovery, orders, service and feedback.

OídoProduct

Our product

One lightweight guest experience, alongside the existing POS.

Merchant

Publish a mobile storefront for menus or services, accessible via QR or a link.

Guest

Browse a multilingual experience with ordering, tips and feedback flows.

Operator

Review activity and learn where onboarding and guest actions create friction.

Current browser beta includes illustrative customer interactions; production payment settlement and paid merchant usage remain to be validated.

OídoAI opportunity

AI development roadmap

From unstructured menu to reviewed, multilingual catalogue.

01 / INPUT

Ingest

PDFs, photos and text supplied by merchants.

02 / EXTRACT

Structure

Multimodal extraction, item categorisation and data normalisation.

03 / REVIEW

Verify

Detect uncertainties and keep merchant approval in the loop.

04 / PUBLISH

Localise

Produce a usable multilingual customer experience.

These AI capabilities are being specified and evaluated. They are not claimed as a deployed production model.

04 / 10
OídoDifferentiation

Our differentiation

Human-reviewed AI in a merchant-first workflow.

Existing-stack fit

No forced replacement of the merchant's point-of-sale system.

Built for multilingual service

Design for international guests and local operators, starting with ES / EN / CA.

Traceable changes

Make AI-suggested content inspectable before customers see it.

Our defensibility hypothesis is the end-to-end merchant workflow, verified data quality and accumulated operational learning — not a claim of proprietary foundation models.

OídoAI validation

Technical validation plan

Make AI quality and cost measurable.

Data & evaluation

Permissioned or synthetic menu samples, ground-truth annotation and failure taxonomy.

Model comparison

OCR and multimodal extraction, structured outputs, translation quality and uncertainty detection.

Operating economics

Measure accuracy, review time, latency, inference cost, privacy and infrastructure needs.
