Questions
owners ask.

What does it cost?

Proposed pilot rates: €79 setup and €39, €79 or €129 a month, before VAT. Exact terms are agreed before onboarding.

Do my clients need an app?

No. They scan the QR code or tap your link and the page opens in the browser.

Do I have to change my till or booking tool?

No. Oído works beside what you already use.

What if my prices change?

Edit a line in the Panel and publish. Your QR codes and link stay the same.
