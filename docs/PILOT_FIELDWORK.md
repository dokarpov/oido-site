# Oído merchant evidence plan — 18 October 2026

## What this study must decide
Can independent, tourist-facing Barcelona cafés and casual restaurants publish and maintain a useful customer-facing offer, generate meaningful guest actions and keep paying without POS replacement or high service costs?

Do not claim that 5–8 Spanish prospects are actual users. They are founder-reported prospective contacts. UAE/Mexico partner and family contacts are discovery access only.

## Pre-demo interview (20–30 min)
Ask for concrete examples from the previous seven days: incorrect menu/service information, missing translation, staff time wasted, customer requests missed, how orders/bookings currently move, which POS/website/WhatsApp tools are already used and paid for. Observe current task without telling them what Oído does. Record baseline frequency, time and whether the buyer controls software spend. Record direct quotes only with permission.

## Evidence definitions (declare before enrolling)
- Invited: identified business accepts pilot terms and obtains a private token.
- Setup complete: real owner reviews source transcription including ambiguous prices, publishes storefront and completes a backend-recorded TEST request.
- Activated: setup complete plus at least one non-test guest request received and accepted or declined by attending staff. Menu-only usage requires a separately preregistered measurement plan: this version does not measure passive scans.
- Repeat use: a merchant acts on non-test requests across two different calendar weeks. Track week two and week four; record missing data rather than pretending no usage.
- Willingness to pay: exact offered tier/price, written merchant acceptance, invoice issued and money collected separately. Interest in demos and simulated tests do not count.
- Support cost: actual operator minutes by merchant and category (onboarding, correction, training, downtime, integration); multiply by real hourly cost.
- Value: only compare to observed baseline; don't equate requests with sales. Actual transactions, refunds, GMV and settlement not available in current candidate.

## 90-day fieldwork schedule
| Period | Activities | Targets (not results) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–14 days | Five owner baseline interviews, two observed setups, verify consent and staff readiness | Publish working first pilot in under 60 minutes median if feasible |
| 15–30 days | Five staffed businesses, observe first guests and changes requested | Count actual non-test requests, staff actions and early support minutes |
| 31–60 days | Expand toward 8–15 merchants if quality passes; repeat interviews | Week-2 behavior, completion, error categories and support per merchant |
| 61–90 days | Make written €39/€79/€129 paid offers to retained merchants | Target ≥5 paid continuations; measure actual acceptance and collections |

Stop/reposition if merchants cannot complete setup without bespoke work, if support cost exceeds plausible subscription value, if guest requests create operational confusion, or if retained merchants consistently refuse the paid offer.

## Per-merchant evidence ledger (not in product database)
Merchant pseudonymous ID; signed study and data terms; sector; current POS/web stack; owner interview and baseline; material source file; number of uncertain lines and corrected prices; import/review/publish time; QR test pass; TEST request count; real submitted and staff-acknowledged counts; week-2/week-4 active dates; errors/support minutes and rate; quoted price accepted?; invoiced?; collected?; cancel reason; participant permission for quotes. Note gaps and attribution.

## Decision
Proceed toward the €150k investment plan only if real merchant activity and paid continuations suggest an economical recurring task. €50k is validation runway, €250k is conditional; neither amount is evidence of demand. Keep POS independence by default and record specific integration requests with their opportunity cost.
